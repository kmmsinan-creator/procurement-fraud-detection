Thesis vs leakage-free AUC

The thesis applied SMOTE before cross-validation, which inflates scores. Leakage-free numbers apply it inside each training fold.

The test labels are hidden, so this ranking cannot be verified. Scores near 1.0 are saturated; use the band, not the decimals.

Limits. Five summary statistics, no dates or suppliers, and only 99 fraud cases, so model differences of about 0.01 AUC are noise. A High band means about ten times the average fraud rate, and most High hospitals are still not labelled fraud. It is a reason to review, not proof.
